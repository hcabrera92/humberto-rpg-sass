◈ CASE STUDY 001

CELERITY CRAFT

How we gamified an ISP product launch inside Minecraft — and packed a physical esports arena to capacity.

UI/UX DESIGN GAMING CULTURE HYBRID EXPERIENCES
01 — THE CHALLENGE

THE DISCONNECT BETWEEN CORPORATE AND CULTURE

Gamers don't just ignore traditional advertising — they actively despise it. When Celerity prepared to launch their new Celerity Gamer Plus internet plan, the product team knew standard corporate ads would fall flat with a community that is fiercely defensive of its spaces.

The mandate was clear but difficult: Introduce a technical utility product to a highly skeptical, ad-blind demographic, build genuine brand affinity, and drive direct traffic to the product landing page.

02 — THE STRATEGY

DESIGNING A PLAYABLE PRODUCT JOURNEY

Operating within an Agile Framework as the team's UI Designer, I recognized that a beautiful interface wouldn't save the launch if the ecosystem lacked cultural authenticity. I stepped beyond the traditional boundaries of a UI role to act as our cross-functional Gaming Culture Specialist, ensuring the entire user journey felt native to the community.

Instead of buying standard ad space, we built Celerity Craft — a custom, branded Minecraft server designed as a playable marketing funnel. My role focused on bridging the digital and physical experience:

  • Gaming-Native UX/UI: Designed the in-game environmental touchpoints and branding, integrating product benefits naturally into the world so they enhanced gameplay instead of interrupting it.
  • The Conversion Funnel: Optimized the seamless transition from the virtual server to high-intent web traffic at gamer.celerity.ec.
  • The Hybrid Bridge: Coordinated the player journey from the digital server to the live grand finale at Kubox Esports Arena, aligning the stream UI with host Iskra Landucci to create a unified experience for both the physical crowd and thousands online.
03 — THE RESULTS

LEGENDARY OUTCOMES

  • 500+ Peak Concurrent Players actively engaged inside the branded server.
  • 80+ Influencers co-streaming the activation simultaneously, multiplying organic reach.
  • Max Capacity Live Audience at Kubox Esports Arena for the hybrid finale.
  • National Press Coverage across 3 major publications, validating the project's cultural impact.
  • High-Intent Business Traffic generated directly to the gamer.celerity.ec landing page.
